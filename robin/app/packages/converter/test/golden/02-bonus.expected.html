Reading club stickers

Synthetic converter fixture about a fictional library activity.

Each finished book earns one paper star. Stars have no monetary value.

  1. Pick a book.
  2. Read at your own pace.
    • Audio books count too.
    • Ask for a large-print edition if useful.
  3. Put a star on the wall chart.

“Every story starts with a page.”

A made-up slogan for this fixture.
