# Use reusable trays

Synthetic decision fixture.

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2030-04-03

## Context

The example seed table needs containers that fit a small shelf.

1. Cardboard boxes — easy to label, difficult to wipe clean.
2. Reusable trays — washable and stackable.

## Decision
